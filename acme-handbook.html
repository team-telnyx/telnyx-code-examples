ACME Corp Employee Handbook

Welcome to ACME Corp. This handbook covers the policies every employee needs to know. It is maintained by People Ops and updated regularly.

Section 3: Time Off

Sick Days

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Unused sick days do not roll over. Notify your manager before 9:00 AM on the day you are sick.

Vacation (PTO)

Employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year, accrued monthly. PTO must be requested through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for a full week off.

Section 4: PTO Carry-Over

Section 4.2: Carry-Over Cap

Employees may carry over up to 5 days of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is paid out at the end of the year. Carry-over days must be used by June 30 of the following year or they are forfeited.

Section 5: Expenses

Section 5.1: Travel and Conferences

Conference attendance requires manager approval. The company reimburses conference flights up to $1,500 per trip. Hotels are reimbursed up to $250 per night. All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the trip in the finance tool.

Section 5.2: Meals and Per Diem

While traveling, employees are reimbursed up to $75 per day for meals. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Section 6: Remote Work

Hybrid Schedule

ACME operates a hybrid model: employees work 3 days per week in the office (Tuesday through Thursday) and may work remotely the remaining 2 days. Fully remote arrangements require VP approval and are reviewed quarterly.

Section 7: Equipment

Employees may purchase work equipment through the IT portal. Equipment purchases are refundable within 30 days of receipt if returned in original packaging. Laptops are replaced every 3 years.
